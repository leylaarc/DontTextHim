Privacy policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Summary

Don't Text Him is designed to work on your device without an account. We do not sell your personal information. Preferences and progress are stored only on your device.

Information we process

The app may store locally on your phone: whether you have completed the intro flow, your selected situation category, which quotes you have seen in the current session (for variety), and drafts you save in the Unsent texts section (including any perspective shown for that draft). This data stays on your device unless you use the optional AI coach described below.

Optional AI perspective

If your app build is connected to a coach API, the text you paste in Unsent texts and your situation label may be sent to that service to generate a short perspective. We do not use that content for advertising. If no coach API is configured, perspective is generated on your device only and nothing is sent over the network for that feature.

Analytics and third parties

The app does not include in-app advertising SDKs or social logins. Apple, Google, or other platform services involved in distributing or updating the app may process data according to their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@donttexthim.app or use the contact information on the app store listing.
